10. Chrome browser extension: AgentR Resume Extractor

This section applies specifically to the AgentR Resume Extractor Chrome browser extension (“the Extension”) and supplements the general privacy policy above.

The Extension operates differently from the AgentR web platform. Specifically:

  • The Extension does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on AgentR’s or Vibencode’s servers.
  • When you use the Extension on a supported job board (e.g. Naukri.com), it reads the candidate listing on your active browser tab solely to identify resume download links. This processing happens entirely within your browser.
  • Resume files selected for download are saved directly to your local machine. They are not uploaded to, routed through, or stored on any of our or any third party servers at any point.
  • The Extension does not run passively in the background. It only activates when you explicitly click the extension icon.
  • The Extension does not use cookies, analytics, or any tracking technology.
  • No login or AgentR account is required to use the Extension.

The general data practices described in sections 2 to 5 of this policy apply to the AgentR web platform (app.agentr.global) but not to the Extension.

As a user of the Extension, you are responsible for handling any downloaded candidate data in accordance with applicable data protection laws in your jurisdiction, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, for users in India.

11. AgentR Guard: Windows desktop application

This section applies specifically to AgentR Guard, a Windows desktop app you run during a live interview, and supplements the general privacy policy above.

What it is

  • A narrow tool that catches cheating software which hides a window from screen sharing so your interviewer cannot see it.
  • You choose when to run it. It is paired to a single interview with a one-time code, and does not run outside of that.

What it does not do

  • It does not record or access your camera, microphone, or screen.
  • It does not take screenshots or log your keystrokes.
  • It cannot read your files, your browsing history, or what is on any window or browser tab.
  • It does not read what you copy:
    • it notices that you copied something, what kind of thing it was (text, an image, or files), and how big it was;
    • it never reads, keeps, or sends the thing itself;
    • if you use a password manager during your interview, your password is never read.

What it looks for on your computer

While running, it reads the following signals from Windows itself, not from anything on your screen:

  • windows hidden from screen sharing;
  • whether your camera, microphone, or speakers are in use, and by which app;
  • whether the screen is being recorded, or a screenshot was just taken;
  • what displays and devices are connected, so a virtual camera can be spotted;
  • whether the computer is a virtual machine or is being remotely controlled;
  • the name of the app currently in front.

Speaker activity is treated only as background context, since Windows cannot tell playback from recording.

What we receive

Every few seconds we receive a short, signed status message containing:

  • an overall result for the interview: clean, review, or suspicious;
  • for anything flagged: the type of signal, how serious it is, and the name of the app involved.

We do not receive window titles, file names, web addresses, screenshots, audio, or anything you have copied. Device and hardware names stay on your computer. We use these signals only to assess the integrity of your interview and share the result with the organisation conducting it.

Storage and logs

  • These signals are stored against your interview record and retained as described in section 6.
  • The app also keeps a small diagnostic log on your own computer, recording things like when it started and whether it connected. The log contains no app names, file names, window titles, or web addresses. It is never uploaded automatically, and we may ask you to send it if you report a problem.

Your choices

  • Running AgentR Guard is a condition set by the organisation conducting your interview, so if you would rather not, please raise it with them beforehand.
  • You can close the app at any time to stop all monitoring immediately. Uninstalling removes it completely.
  • It does not ask Windows for camera or microphone permission, because it never uses either.
