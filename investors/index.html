for investors

Not software with some AI features.

We are rebuilding hiring from first principles. The resume stopped being a signal, and every tool in the category is still ranking them faster.

  • The bottleneck moved from reading applications to trusting them.
  • We built the verification layer, not another ranking layer.
  • Three markets want the same product for three different reasons.

Why now.

  • the signal
    Anyone can write a senior-sounding career in nine seconds

    A language model can make any resume read like the right one. It cannot invent an outcome that never happened. Every screening tool built before 2023 assumes the document is evidence. It is now a draft.

  • the tooling
    Applicant tracking rewards the people who studied the filter

    Keyword matching hands the shortlist to whoever read a blog post about resume optimisation, not to whoever did the work. Faster ranking of the same bad signal does not fix this.

  • the answer
    Read everything, then check what it claims

    Reading is cheap now. Verification is the scarce thing, and it is the part nobody shipped. That is the layer we built, and it is the reason the product is defensible.

What is already running.

Not a roadmap. This is the system, in production, today.

Every application read

Parsed into structured fields, contact details stripped before scoring, hidden prompt injection removed and retained. Nothing is filtered out before a human sees it.

Claims checked against the public record

Roles and achievements are searched against public sources and returned with one of six verdicts and the reasoning behind each. This is the piece competitors do not have.

Structured interviews at the bar you set

Built from the role, scored against a rubric written in advance, taken on the candidate’s own time. No calendars, no interviewer in the room.

Integrity, honestly scoped

Browser signals are advisory. A native macOS Guard can hold the room shut. Neither ever moves a score, and nothing auto-rejects. We would rather say that than imply certainty.

Three markets, one product.

The same system sells three different ways, which is what makes the wedge workable rather than scattered.

India: volume

Two hundred applicants for one seat is normal. The pitch is that nobody gets skimmed and nobody gets auto-rejected, at any volume.

The West: fit

Smaller piles, higher cost per bad hire. The pitch is evidence: every rank opens into the line of the resume that earned it.

The Middle East: verification

Credential checking is a standing requirement, not a nice-to-have. The claim check is the product, and it already exists.
