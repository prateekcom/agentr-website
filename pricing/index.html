How credit works.

One balance, spent by the work rather than by the seat. It is the same meter we run against ourselves, which is why you can see it.

  • one balance
    Reading, checking and interviewing all draw from it

    Every piece of AI work the system does is metered and subtracted. Heavier roles cost more than light ones, because they genuinely are more work. Nothing is charged per seat or per user.

  • a period
    A plan runs for a fixed window

    Credit sits inside a validity period with a start and an end date. Both are shown in the product, next to what is left.

  • in the open
    The balance is not hidden behind a sales call

    Plan, validity, credit remaining and status sit in your settings, updated as you go. You should never have to ask us how much you have left.

  • not billed
    Some background work is on us

    Research we run about your organisation to set the system up is excluded from your balance. You are billed for work on your candidates, not for us learning about you.

Questions about the bill.

What actually spends credit?
The AI work: reading and scoring an application, checking a claim against the public record, running and grading an interview. Ordinary use of the product, opening a role, reading a report, reordering a list, does not.
What happens when the credit runs out?
New AI work stops. The product does not lock: your existing reports, shortlists, evidence and recordings stay exactly where they are, and reading, reordering and re-ranking keep working. Hitting a limit should never take away what you already paid for.
Does a heavy role cost more than a light one?
Yes. Six hundred applicants is more reading than sixty, and the meter reflects that. We would rather say so than pretend volume is free and price it in somewhere you cannot see.
Does credit expire?
A plan has a validity window, so credit sits inside a period rather than lasting forever. The dates are in your settings from the day you start.
Do you need a card to start?
No card for the free credit, and no sales call unless you ask for one.
