who is behind this

We built the tool we needed.

AgentR started as a complaint, not a company. It is still mostly a complaint, just one with an evaluation pipeline attached.

We once spent three weekends reading two hundred resumes for one backend role. Half of them claimed to have led the migration. Two of them actually had. By Sunday afternoon of the third weekend nobody was reading carefully any more, and we all knew it.

The tools we tried made it worse. They ranked people by keyword bingo, which meant the candidates who had read a blog post about resume optimisation beat the candidates who had done the work. That is not a filter. That is a lottery with extra steps.

So we built the thing we wished existed. It reads every application properly, against criteria you write down. It checks what people claim against the public record. It shows its work, every time, with the line of the resume that earned the score attached.

You still make every decision. You just stop doing the paperwork.

Three things we refuse to move on.

  • 01
    Evidence beats polish

    A language model can make any career sound senior in nine seconds. It cannot invent an outcome that never happened. So a bare skills list earns nothing, and a result with a number still attached earns a lot.

  • 02
    The human decides, always

    Nothing is auto-rejected. The whole pile reaches you, ordered, weak end included. Where to stop reading is a judgement call, and judgement calls belong to people.

  • 03
    Show the working

    Every score carries the line that earned it. Every flagged claim carries its sources. If we cannot show you why, we do not get to assert it.
